# Misère Perdue (MISERY) — site guide

The working manual for misereperdue.com: how it's built, how it looks, how it
deploys, and how to change it safely. Written by Grim (Grok Bot) on 2026-10-22
when taking over from the previous bot (Muse, git author "Ryuk").

> **Mobile first.** dc reviews everything on his phone, and most visitors use the
> Home Screen web app. Every change is designed, tested and screenshotted at phone
> sizes first: **iPhone 390×844** and **small Android 360×780**, with touch emulation,
> in **both dark and light mode**. That includes the splash, the service worker,
> the shop grid, the auth tabs and the forum. Desktop (1440×900) comes second.

## 3. Design system

### Colour

Theme follows the phone's system setting. `js/theme.js` sets `data-theme="dark|light"`
on `<html>` and updates `<meta name="theme-color">` (`#070f29` dark, `#ffffff` light).
The tokens live at the top of `src/css/site.css`:

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` / `--bg-2` | `#ffffff` | `#070f29` (deep navy) | page background |
| `--ink` | `#1c1c1e` | `#f5f5f7` | text |
| `--muted` | `#636366` | `#b7bdd0` | secondary text |
| `--accent` | `#0a1230` (**Misery navy**) | `#a9d7f5` (**ice blue**) | links, active states, buttons |
| `--wash` | `rgba(10,18,48,.07)` | `rgba(169,215,245,.13)` | soft background glows |
| `--fill` | `#f4f4f6` | `rgba(255,255,255,.06)` | inputs, chips |
| `--stroke` / `--hairline` | `rgba(0,0,0,.08)` | `rgba(255,255,255,.28)` / `.10` | borders |
| `--glass-*` | white tints | navy/graphite tints | "liquid glass" bars and cards: `blur(12–24px) saturate(150–160%)`, 0.5px hairline, 1px inset specular |

Brand constants: **`#0a1230`** is the splash, app-icon background, PWA
`background_color` and `theme_color`. Ice blue `#a9d7f5` is the dark-mode accent.
Status colours: green `#34c759`, red `#ff453a` / `#ff6b6b`, gold `#f5b301` / `#ffd60a` (stars and VIP).
The static SEO pages use their own light style (`#1a1a2e` text, `#2b4eff` links).

### Type

- UI: the system stack, `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Text", "SF Pro Display", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`, at line-height 1.4.
- **Monospace accents**: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace`. Used for product captions (title/price under shop tiles), the "MISERY" kicker on the Globe tile and the forum search placeholder.
- Headline example: Globe tile title is 30px bold, with the kicker in letter-spaced mono.

### Shape and spacing

- Pills everywhere (`border-radius: 999px`): nav, tabs, chips, buttons.
- Cards are 12–22px radius (14 and 22 are common). Circles are 50% (avatars).
- Phone gutters are about 16–20px. Auth card top padding is 12px (dc asked for it tighter).

### Signature motifs (dc has iterated on all of these; ask before changing)

- **Hero**: a looping header video (`video/header-ping.mp4`, poster `video/header.jpg`) of chrome/silver ornaments behind a floating glass **nav pill**. The nav has Store (bag), Globe and Chat icons (Phosphor-style SVGs), plus a bag/cart button with a badge. The active tab gets a sliding `.seg` highlight that is measured in pixels.
- **Splash**: a navy `#0a1230` full screen with **the eyes** (a traced pair of white eyes). It shows on the first load of a session and on pull-to-refresh, and **blinks once** on the refresh splash (shut at about 450ms, open by about 600ms, lifted at 850ms). There is a safety cap of 1150ms. It respects reduced motion. iOS startup images `img/splash-*.png` carry the same eyes (pins `?v=24`).
- **App icon**: the eyes plus a frown on a charcoal background (`apple-touch-icon-fresh.png`, `img/icon-192/512.png`, `img/apple-touch-icon.png`). Pins: `?v=11` in manifests, `?v=26` in the SW.
- **Shop "photobook" grid**: two columns of **portrait 4:5 photo tiles** with a monospace title and price caption underneath. Grid tracks are `minmax(0,1fr)` with `min-width:0` cards, so long titles can't stretch a column.
- **Misery Globe promo tile**: leads the grid and spans both columns. It is about 12:5, navy glass, with the "MISERY" kicker, the title "Globe", "The board" and a "Get an account →" link that opens sign-in. A **spinning wireframe globe** on the right is an SVG texture scrolling sideways inside a shaded circle (9s linear loop, reduced-motion respected).
- **Auth card** (Chat tab, logged out): a segmented **Sign in / Sign up** slider. **One** pill slides under the active button. It is sized from the button's pixel box (`offsetWidth/Left`) and refit with a `ResizeObserver`, using no-overshoot easing. Below it are username/password fields, a navy primary button, a black **Continue with X** button and a Cloudflare **Turnstile** check.
- **Forum (Globe)**: a header card with Sign in, round category chips (emoji icons), a search field, and thread cards. Each card has a title, author chip (verified seal for dc, star badge, VIP), meta line, excerpt and a photo thumbnail that fades into the card's blurred wash (multi-stop left fade plus a top/bottom melt). Tag chips sit underneath, and pinned threads get a red pin.
- **Motion**: pills slide, menus pop (`menuPop`, `reactPickerIn`), messages pop in (`chat-msg-in`), neon/orbit rings mark "live", and the VIP seal shines. **Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.**
- **3D**: `mask-3d.html` and `vip-card.html` use three.js 0.160 from jsDelivr (importmap) with front/back/bump textures in `img/`.

## 4. JavaScript architecture

Load order (`index.html`):
1. Inline head script: builds the splash at parse time, locks scroll while it shows, and blocks pinch/double-tap zoom.
2. `css/site.css?v=…`, `js/theme.js?v=…`, then an analytics beacon (`POST /api/analytics` on load).
3. `js/chat.js?v=…` (sync). Defines `window.MPChat`: the API helper, auth, chat UI, WebSocket and push.
4. A small loader (`loadOne(src)` with one retry) pulls the rest in parallel:
   - `dashboard-v2.js` (owner dashboard/stats), `social-logos.js`, `ph-icons.js`
   - the **shop chain**: `shopify.js` + `cart.js` + `vip3d.js` + `mask3d.js`, then `shop.js` (`window.MPShop`)
   - the **forum chain**: `forum3.js`, then `forum-mount-patch.js`
5. Inline router: hash → panel, `MPShop.renderShop()`, `openProduct()`, etc. It also fetches the checkout pay badges from `images/pay-*.png.b64` (keep those files).

| File | Global | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `js/chat.js` | `MPChat` | Auth (username/password + Turnstile, **Continue with X** OAuth 2 PKCE via `/api/x/*`), session token in `localStorage.chat_token`, device id `chat_device`. Live chat over `wss://api.misereperdue.com/api/ws` (ticket from `/api/ws-ticket`). Reactions, mentions, notifications, **Web Push** subscription (VAPID public key, `/api/push/subscribe`) |
| `js/forum3.js` | — | The Misery Globe forum: `/api/forum/threads`, `/thread/<id>`, `/categories`, `/tags`, editor, replies, media upload (`/api/media`) |
| `js/forum-mount-patch.js` | — | Small fixes on top of forum3 (editor mount, loading spinner) |
| `js/dashboard-v2.js` | — | Owner/admin dashboard pieces (stats, moderation) using `MPChat.api` |
| `js/shopify.js` *(readable, edit in place)* | `MPShopify` | Storefront API client for `shop.misereperdue.com`. The **public** Storefront token is public by design |
| `js/cart.js` | `MP` | Cart in `localStorage.mp_cart`, money formatting (CAD) |
| `js/shop.js` | `MPShop` | Shop grid (photobook + Globe tile), product view, bag panel, checkout. It creates a Shopify cart and redirects only to `shop.misereperdue.com`, `*.myshopify.com` or `checkout.shopify.com` |
| `js/mask3d.js`, `js/vip3d.js` | `MPMask3D`, `MPVip3D` | Inline 3D product previews |
| `js/theme.js`, `js/ph-icons.js`, `js/social-logos.js`, `js/uui-icons.js` *(readable)* | | Theme, icon sets (`uui-icons.js` is currently unreferenced) |
| `sw.js` *(readable)* | | Service worker (below) |

**Membership/VIP**: buying the VIP product on Shopify triggers the webhook
`POST /api/shopify/order-paid` (HMAC verified with `SHOPIFY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`). The Worker then upgrades the account and sends a "VIP" push.

### Service worker and versioning (critical)

`sw.js` precaches the app shell into cache `mp-shell-v<SW_VERSION>`. On activate it deletes every other cache.

- **HTML / navigations**: network-first, with the cache as offline fallback. HTML changes land on the next load, with no version bump needed.
- **Anything with `?v=`**: **cache-first and immutable**. A file is never re-fetched while its `?v=` is unchanged.
- Other same-origin static files: stale-while-revalidate.
- `/api/*`, cross-origin and non-GET requests: never cached.
- Push: shows "Misery" notifications, dedupes ids, and sets the app badge. Clicking opens `/#globe/<id>` or `/#journal`.

Therefore, **whenever a JS/CSS/image file changes you must**:
1. Bump that file's `?v=N` in `index.html`, and in `sw.js` `SHELL` if it is listed there.
2. Bump `SW_VERSION` in `sw.js` **and** the matching `register("/sw.js?v=N")` in `index.html` (they must be equal; `npm run check` enforces it).

Muse's pin counters on 2026-10-22: css `571`, chat `284`, forum3 `1106`, shop `68`, SW `83`.

## 5. Backend: the `misery-chat` Worker

- A backup of its config is in `worker/misery-chat/wrangler.toml`: Durable Object `CHAT_ROOM` → class `ChatRoom` (SQLite), custom domain `api.misereperdue.com`, compatibility date `2024-01-01`, no crons/KV/D1/R2.
- Secrets (names only): `AGENT_KEY`, `GROQ_API_KEY`, `OWNER_SECRET`, `SHOPIFY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `TURNSTILE_SECRET`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `X_CLIENT_ID`, `X_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- Main routes: `/api/register|login|logout|me|profile|password`, `/api/x/*` (X OAuth), `/api/ws`, `/api/ws-ticket`, `/api/messages|send|react|delete`, `/api/forum/*`, `/api/media/*`, `/api/notifications*`, `/api/push/*`, `/api/analytics`, `/api/shopify/order-paid`, `/api/agent/*` (bot actions with `X-Agent-Key`), plus owner/admin routes (`ban`, `badge`, `chat/pause`, …).
- CORS allows only `https://misereperdue.com` and `https://www.misereperdue.com`. **Pages previews (`*.pages.dev`) cannot log in or post.** On a preview, check anything that needs the API on production after merge, or test against production with local files (see §7).
- It was last deployed 2026-10-21 via the API/dashboard. It is **not** deployed from this repo. Never redeploy it without dc's explicit OK.
- `worker/legacy/comments.js` is the original Oct-8 backend. It is not deployed; it's kept for history.

## 7. Per-change checklist

dc usually asks for a change in chat, often with a screenshot or screen recording.
Muse's commits are titled "<what> per dc: <detail>; <pins bumped>". Keep that style.

1. **Branch**: `git switch -c grim/<short-name>` from fresh `main`. Never push straight to `main`.
2. **Edit the readable source**: `src/js/*.js` or `src/css/site.css`; HTML, `sw.js` and readable JS in place.
3. **Build**: `npm install` once, then `npm run build`. Only changed outputs are rewritten.
4. **Bump versions**: the `?v=` of every changed asset in `index.html` (and in `sw.js` SHELL), then `SW_VERSION` in `sw.js` and the matching `sw.js?v=` in `index.html`. HTML-only changes need no bump.
5. **Check**: `npm run check && npm run verify`. These must pass; the warnings explain themselves.
6. **Mock-render on phones first**: headless Chrome at 390×844 and 360×780 (touch, DPR 3), dark **and** light, then desktop. Use the screenshot script (`/workspace/tools/shoot.mjs` on the bot box; it can serve local files under the real domain so the API works). Compare against current production screenshots, and look at the splash, shop grid, auth tabs and forum.
7. **Push the branch and open a PR.** GitHub Actions `check` runs, and Cloudflare Pages builds a preview at `https://<branch-with-dashes>.misereperdue.pages.dev`. Verify the preview on phone viewports: every page loads, no new console errors, assets load. Remember the API rejects `pages.dev` origins (see §5).
8. **Merge to `main`** only when the preview is clean and dc has OK'd the change. Pages deploys in about 15 s.
9. **Verify production on phone viewports**: hard-load, confirm the new `sw.js?v=` is active (DevTools → Application → Service Workers, or `navigator.serviceWorker.controller.scriptURL`), and confirm the cache name is `mp-shell-v<N>`.
10. **If something breaks**: revert the merge commit on `main` (Pages redeploys), or roll back in Cloudflare → Pages → Deployments → "Rollback to this deployment".

SEO pages: after forum activity, run `npm run sitemap` (`python3 build-sitemap.py`,
GETs the public API only) and commit the changed `t/*.html` and `sitemap.xml`. Product
pages in `p/` are hand-edited. Keep their `<title>`, canonical, `og:url` and JSON-LD `url` in clean form.

## 8. Known issues and open decisions (as of 2026-10-22)

- **Pageview analytics never arrive**: the `sendBeacon` in `index.html` posts `application/json` with credentials. That needs a CORS preflight, which fails, so `/api/analytics` gets nothing. Fix: send the Blob as `text/plain` (the Worker parses JSON regardless).
- `index.html` calls `MPShop.updateCartBadge()` before `shop.js` has loaded, which throws `ReferenceError: MPShop is not defined` on every load (harmless, but noisy). Guard it with `window.MPShop &&`.
- `sw.js` precaches `forum3.js?v=1096` while `index.html` loads `?v=1106`. That's a wasted precache; fix it on the next SW bump.
- CSP is **report-only**. If it's ever enforced, add `https://challenges.cloudflare.com` to `frame-src` (Turnstile), `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net` to `script-src` (three.js), and `data:` handling for Turnstile.
- Owner decisions are pending (not changed by the bot): CSP enforcement, HSTS length/preload, DNSSEC, CAA records, Bot Fight Mode, GitHub Pages being on, and whether to keep the Worker source in this public repo.
