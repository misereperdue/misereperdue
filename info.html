About this site

Misère Perdue (Misery) is an independent clothing and merch brand with a community around it. The site includes chat, the Misery Globe forum, misery fm, and digital items tied to your account: Stars, Powers, and a VIP membership.

Payments

Purchases on the site are processed by Whop. When you pay, you also agree to Whop’s Buyer Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which are shown at checkout. We never see or store your full card details.

Prices are in Canadian dollars. Any sales tax, and any currency conversion if you pay in another currency, is calculated by Whop and shown before you pay. Promo codes, where offered, are applied at checkout.

Your purchase is added to your account only after our server confirms the payment with Whop. Sign in before you buy so it lands on the right account.

Order emails

We send order emails from orders@misereperdue.com to the email address you enter at checkout: a receipt when you buy Stars, VIP or clothing, a receipt each time VIP renews, and a confirmation if you cancel VIP. You can reply to these emails with questions about an order.

All sales are final

Except where the law requires otherwise, every purchase is final. We do not offer refunds, returns, or exchanges on merch, Stars, Powers, VIP, or any other purchase.

If the law in your area gives you a right you cannot waive, that right still applies.

If a payment is refunded or disputed, what it paid for is removed: Stars from that purchase are taken back (never below a zero balance) and VIP from that payment ends.

Stars and Powers

Stars are sold in packs (500, 1,100, 2,400 and 6,500 stars) and are used to buy Powers and send gifts on the site. Stars and Powers are digital. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and are not transferable, except where the site lets you gift Stars. We may change, limit, or remove Stars, Powers, or how they work, at any time.

VIP

VIP is a subscription billed through Whop. The first charge is CA$13.98, then CA$3.99 every 30 days until you cancel, plus any tax Whop calculates. The current price is always shown before you pay.

You can cancel at any time from your profile settings or from the Whop billing link in your VIP emails. After you cancel you will not be charged again, and your VIP access lasts until the end of the period you already paid for. We do not give prorated refunds for unused time, except where the law requires it. If a renewal payment fails, VIP ends when the paid period runs out.

Clothing

Clothing drops are announced on the site. Items marked Preorder can be bought now and ship on the date shown (the Enraged Beanie and V_V Mask ship in September 2027). Add them to your bag and tap Purchase: the payment sheet asks for your shipping address, then takes payment through Whop. You get a confirmation email, and another when your order ships. Items marked with a countdown are not on sale yet.

Your username, password (stored only in a scrambled, one-way form), profile details, and what you post or upload: chat messages, private messages, forum posts, reactions, photos and music. Purchases. Whop handles payment and receives your payment details and the email you enter at checkout. To run your account, the shop and the community, to deliver what you buy, to send order emails and the notifications you asked for, to keep the site secure, and to remove content or accounts that break these terms.
