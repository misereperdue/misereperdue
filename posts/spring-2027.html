Spring 2027 is the date

The old page was a black screen and a line. That stays the front door. Behind it, the site is now a journal and a store.

Spring 2027 is still the season. Pieces in the store are samples until a real cut is locked. Prices are in Canadian dollars. Orders land as notes, not charges — payment is not switched on yet.

The book has its own page. Title is open. Cover is a black cloth book until the real one is shot.
